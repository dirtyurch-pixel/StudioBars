NOTICE for backend/app/phonetics/data/frequency_en.tsv
=======================================================

This file is a DERIVED WORK: the 60,000 most frequent English words from the "large" English
word list of the `wordfreq` package (version 3.1.1, by Robyn Speer), each with its Zipf
frequency value. It is made with scripts/build_frequency_data.py.
